# Mock Board demo video: script and shot list

Target **2:40** (guideline 2–3 minutes). Draft of Nov 7, 2026, for the owner's approval before any
paid call.

## Ground rules (from CLAUDE.md, the brief and the contest rules)

- **Everything the board does is a real, live run.** Prisma hears, Nova decides, Timbre speaks. No
  board line, caption, number or report text is typed in by hand or re-recorded.
- **The candidate is fictional and their voice is generated by Gnani Timbre.** The video says so
  on screen the whole time the candidate speaks. It is not presented as a human recording.
- **Editing only removes waiting time** between turns. A small badge says "Pauses shortened ·
  response times are measured". The response times shown come from the session's own events.
- **If the board mishears or stumbles, the cut keeps it** (the brief: "show the repair question
  rather than hiding the failure"). The candidate's request lines below are phrases the board's
  rules were checked to catch, offline, before writing them.
- **Tamil is labelled** "not yet reviewed by a fluent Tamil speaker".
- Name every Gnani model: **Prisma (prisma-v2.5)** and **Timbre (timbre-v2.5)**; the board's
  reasoning is **Amazon Nova Pro**.
- No UPSC emblem, no claim of accuracy that was not measured, no partnership implied.

## Shot list

### 1. Cold open (0:00–0:10)
**Screen:** black. The particle field gathers into the brain. The chairperson's real first question
from the Hinglish sitting plays, and its caption types on in time with the audio. Then the title:
**Mock Board** / *The UPSC interview, spoken.*
**Audio:** the board's real voice, then a low tone under the title.

### 2. The problem (0:10–0:24)
**Narrator:** "Aspirants practise for the UPSC Personality Test by writing answers. But the
interview is spoken. A board listens, interrupts, and follows up on what you just said. Writing
can't rehearse that."
**Screen:** kinetic type. "Written practice" sits silent; "A real board" pulses with each phrase:
*listens · interrupts · follows up*.

### 3. Meet the board (0:24–0:40)
**Narrator:** "Mock Board is a five-member practice panel that speaks Hinglish, Hindi, English and
Tamil. You fill a short form, with no name and no roll number, pick a language, and check your
microphone."
**Screen:** the real form being filled (fast, smooth), the language list opening, then the real
microphone check result from Prisma ("The board heard you clearly"). Callouts: *No name field* ·
*4 languages* · *Mic check by Gnani Prisma*.

### 4. The Hinglish sitting (0:40–1:35), the heart of the video
**On-screen label, held throughout:** "Fictional candidate · voice generated by Gnani Timbre ·
the board is live".
**Narrator (one line, over the opening):** "From here, nothing is scripted on the board's side."
**Screen:** the live room, slightly zoomed, with the **live tracking panel** sliding in on the
right. For each turn it shows, as it happens:

```
HEARD        what Prisma wrote down
UNDERSTOOD   answer · request: repeat · request: change topic · request: end
DECISION     follow up · repeat · next member · confirm end
NEXT         which member speaks
RESPONSE     end of speech → board's first sound, in ms (measured)
```

**Turns (candidate lines are fixed; the board's lines are whatever it really says):**

1. *Board:* opening question from the form (real).
   *Candidate:* "Sir, Almora mein maine dekha ki kisaan mehnat bahut karte hain, lekin mandi tak
   pahunchte pahunchte unka faayda kam ho jaata hai. Farmer producer company mein do saal kaam
   karke samjha ki sahi niti zameen par kitna badlav la sakti hai. Isliye main civil services
   mein aana chahta hoon."
2. *Board:* follow-up (real). **Callout:** if the follow-up uses words the candidate said, the
   words are highlighted in both lines ("Follow-up built on: *farmer producer company*"). If it
   does not, no callout is drawn.
   *Candidate:* "Sorry sir, phir se boliye?" → panel: *request: repeat (found by the rules)* →
   the board repeats the question.
3. *Candidate:* "Ji. Hamari company ne kai gaon ke kisaanon ko jod kar seedha khareedaar se baat
   ki. Pehla kadam tha bharosa banana, kyunki kisaan pehle ghaate se darte the. Lekin chunauti
   yeh rahi ki chhote kisaan ke paas bhandaran nahi hota."
4. *Candidate:* "Sir, kya hum kisi aur topic pe baat karte hain?" → panel: *request: change topic*
   → the next member takes over (the seat lights up).
5. *Board:* new member's question (real).
   *Candidate:* "Mujhe lagta hai ki pehle zameeni sthiti samajhni chahiye, phir logon se baat karke
   hal nikalna chahiye. Iska nuksaan yeh ho sakta hai ki faisle mein samay lage, lekin logon ka
   bharosa banta hai."
6. *Candidate:* "Dhanyavaad sir, kya hum interview yahin khatam kar sakte hain?" → the board asks
   to confirm, aloud → *Candidate:* "Haan sir, please end it." → the report is prepared.

**Badge (bottom left, small):** "Pauses shortened · response times are measured".

### 5. The same board, in Tamil (1:35–2:00)
**Narrator (Tamil, Timbre):** "இப்போது அதே குழு, தமிழில்." (Now the same panel, in Tamil.)
**Label:** "Tamil not yet reviewed by a fluent Tamil speaker".
**Screen:** a second, short live sitting in Tanglish, English subtitles under every line, tracking
panel on.
1. *Board:* opening question (real, Tamil).
   *Candidate:* "என் ஊர் மதுரை. Civil engineering படிக்கும்போது தண்ணீர் பிரச்சனை எவ்வளவு பெரியது
   என்று புரிந்தது. அதனால் தான் public service-க்கு வர விரும்புகிறேன்."
2. *Board:* follow-up (real).
   *Candidate:* "முதலில் உள்ளூர் மக்களிடம் பேசுவேன், ஏனென்றால் அவர்களுக்குத்தான் பிரச்சனை நன்றாகத்
   தெரியும். ஆனால் செலவு ஒரு பெரிய சவால்."
3. *Candidate:* "நேர்காணலை இங்கே முடித்துக்கொள்ளலாமா?" → confirm → "ஆமாம் சார், இங்கே முடிக்கலாம்."
   (Only the first exchange may make the final cut, for length.)

### 6. The report (2:00–2:25)
**Narrator:** "When the sitting ends, the report shows exactly how the board heard you: each answer,
what it understood, and any word it probably misheard. Every score quotes your own words. Then
you can answer your weakest question again, aloud, and see what changed."
**Screen:** the real report from the Hinglish sitting. Smooth scroll with three focus moments:
*How the board heard you* (one row opening), *a score with its exact quote*, *the spoken second
attempt* and its comparison ("This time you gave a reason…").

### 7. Under the hood, and close (2:25–2:42)
**Narrator:** "Gnani Prisma hears every answer. Gnani Timbre gives each member a voice. Amazon Nova
Pro decides what the board asks next. Mock Board: practise the interview the way it really
happens."
**Screen:** animated diagram: *your voice → Prisma → the board (Nova) → Timbre → the board's
voice*, with the model names. End card:
*Mock Board · built for Gnani.ai's Great Indian AI Internship Challenge*
*Not affiliated with UPSC · fictional candidate · generated voices*
