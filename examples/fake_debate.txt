Main motion ke paksh mein hoon. Phones class mein dhyan bhatkate hain, kyunki notifications aur games teacher se zyada attention kheenchte hain. Saath hi cyberbullying ka khatra bhi school tak aa jaata hai.
Lekin phones learning tools tabhi hain jab sahi use ho, aur Class 10 tak ke bachche khud ko rok nahi paate kyunki unka self-control abhi ban raha hai. School library aur computer lab yeh kaam bina distraction ke kar sakte hain, isliye phone ki zaroorat nahi hai.
Parents ke liye school office ka landline aur teacher ka number kaafi hai. Fair point ki emergency mein contact zaroori hai, lekin uske liye har bachche ki jeb mein smartphone hona zaroori nahi.
Closing mein main kahunga ki padhai ka samay padhai ke liye hona chahiye. Ban ek saaf rule hai, aur responsible use ghar par sikhaya ja sakta hai, kyunki school ka kaam focus dena hai.
