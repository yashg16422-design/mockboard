The interview, spoken.

Mock Board is a practice interview for the UPSC Personality Test. You speak. A small panel replies, out loud, in Hinglish, Hindi or English.

This is a student's practice tool, built for the Gnani.ai Great Indian AI Internship Challenge. It is not run by the Union Public Service Commission, and it does not use the Commission's emblem. A report from this board is not a UPSC mark.

How a session works

  1. 01

    Form Home state, studies, hobbies. No name, roll number, phone number, email or college.

  2. 02

    Your voice The microphone stays on this page. Gnani Prisma writes down what you say.

  3. 03

    The board Amazon Nova Pro chooses the next line. Gnani Timbre speaks it.

  4. 04

    Report At the end you get a practice report. It is not a UPSC mark.

  1. 01

    Motion The motion is fixed when the app starts. You are told which side is yours.

  2. 02

    Your voice The microphone stays on this page. Gnani Prisma writes down what you say.

  3. 03

    The other side Amazon Nova Pro answers. Gnani Timbre speaks that answer.

  4. 04

    Report At the end you get a practice report of the exchange. It is not a score from any institution.

Headphones. Without them the board can hear itself through your microphone and may cut itself off. You can speak over the board to interrupt it.

Recording is on: your microphone audio (not the board's voice) is saved on this computer in the traces folder, so you can compare transcription settings later. It is never uploaded. Restart the app without --record-audio to turn it off.
