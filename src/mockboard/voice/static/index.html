[ 01 / UPSC Personality Test — practice ]

The interview, spoken.

Mock Board is a practice interview for the UPSC Personality Test. You speak. A small panel replies, out loud, in Hinglish, Tanglish, Hindi or English.

It listens to how you answer,
and the board asks back.

This is a student's practice tool, built for the Gnani.ai Great Indian AI Internship Challenge. It is not run by the Union Public Service Commission, and it does not use the Commission's emblem. A report from this board is not a UPSC mark.

What happens to your words. Your speech goes to Gnani Prisma to be written down. The form and the transcript go to Amazon Bedrock (Nova Pro, Sydney region) so the board can choose its next line. This server keeps a text record of the sitting (what was heard, the board's lines and the report) so faults can be found. Your audio is not saved unless a recording notice is shown on the interview screen. Use made-up details if you prefer.
Headphones. Without them the board can hear itself through your microphone and may cut itself off. You can speak over the board to interrupt it.

[ 02 / Choose how to practise ]

Three ways to practise, out loud.

Each one listens to what you actually say and answers it. Pick one; you can come back and try another.

[ 02 / The motion ]

Choose your motion.

You speak for one side. The board takes the other, out loud, and a judge gives a verdict at the end.

Debate setup

Your side

To hear the board again, say: “Phir se boliye.” To think first, say: “Ek minute sochne dijiye.”

Use headphones, so the board does not hear itself.

[ 02 / Study partner ]

What are you studying?

Explain it the way you would to a sharp friend. Your partner restates your point, asks one good question at a time, and offers the strongest counterpoint, so your own points get stronger. No scores.

Your topic

Take your time: pauses do not end your turn. To think first, say: “Ek minute sochne dijiye.” To finish, say: “Session yahin khatam karte hain.”

Nothing is scored. The summary keeps your own words.

[ 02 / Before you enter ]

Before you enter.

No name, roll number, phone number, email or college. Only what a board would ask about.

Candidate form

Hobbies, up to 3
01 Microphone and language check

Before you begin, read one short line in the language you chose. You will see what the board heard, so you can fix the microphone or the language now rather than mid-interview. Nothing is saved.

02 Likely questions

Questions the board prepares from this form. It may word them differently and it will ask follow-ups, so use them to practise, not to memorise.

    To change topics, say: “Kisi aur topic pe baat karte hain.” To hear a question again, say: “Phir se boliye.” To think before answering, say: “Ek minute sochne dijiye.”
